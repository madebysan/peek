# Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

Peek does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.

When the privacy overlay is active, Peek reads the current cursor position so the clear viewing area can follow the pointer. This happens entirely on your Mac. Cursor positions are not stored or sent anywhere.

Your overlay style, circle size, edge style, keyboard shortcut, and launch-at-login choice are stored locally on your Mac. Peek does not have access to the content beneath its overlay.

Peek can open its privacy policy and project page in your default browser. The app itself does not make network requests.

Questions about this policy can be sent through [santiagoalonso.com](https://santiagoalonso.com).
