# Peek Support

Need help with Peek? This page covers the most common questions and explains how to contact support.

## Quick help

- **Open Peek's controls:** Click the eye icon in the menu bar.
- **Turn the privacy overlay on or off:** Choose **Turn Peek On** or **Turn Peek Off**, or press Option-Command-P.
- **Change the overlay:** Open **Settings** from the menu.
- **Quit Peek:** Choose **Quit Peek** from the menu or use the **Quit Peek** button in Settings.

If the eye icon is not visible, open Peek again from your Applications folder. Peek is a menu bar app, so it does not show a Dock icon.

## Ask a question or report a problem

[Open a support request on GitHub](https://github.com/madebysan/peek/issues/new). Include your Peek version, macOS version, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots are helpful when the problem is visual.

Peek does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.
